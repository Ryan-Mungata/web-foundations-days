About QUICKNOTES

QUICKNOTES is a simple and efficient note-taking tool for capturing your ideas, reminders and to-dos in real-time. It keeps everything on one clean page so you cancapture a thought in seconds and get back to what you were doing.

How to use QUICKNOTES

  1. Type your idea into the "New Note" field.
  2. Click the "Create Note" button to save your note.
  3. Check the "Saved notes" section to view your saved notes.
  4. Watch the note counter to see how many notes you have.

Features

  • Quick note entry with a single input field.
  • A running count of your notes.
  • A clean, distraction-free list of every note saved.
  • Easy navigation between the home and about pages.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + K Jump to the new note field
Ctrl + Enter Add the note you are typing
Esc Clear the new note field
